Getting started

  • Barcode – the fastest and most accurate way, for records that have one.
  • Label – fit the center label inside the circle, hold still and tap Capture & search.
  • Photo – check records you photographed earlier.
  • Manual – type catalog number, label, artist or country (all optional).

Prices by condition

Without an account you can search and see the lowest price for sale. To see Discogs suggested prices for each grade (M, NM, VG+…), tap Sign in with Discogs in Settings and fill in your Discogs seller settings once (currency is enough – you don't need to sell anything).

Tips for label scans

  • Get close: the label should fill the circle.
  • Tilt the record slightly to avoid reflections.
  • Always check the matrix/runout codes in the record page to confirm the exact pressing.
