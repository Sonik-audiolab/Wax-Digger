Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short

  • Wax Digger does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • No accounts with AudioLab, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Photos and camera images are processed only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • The app talks only to the Discogs API, to search records and show prices.

Who we are

Wax Digger is developed by AudioLab (Giuseppe Boragina), Arico, Tenerife, Spain. Contact: audiolabtenerife@gmail.com.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a barcode or a record label. When you pick a photo from your library, iOS gives the app only that single photo.

Barcodes and label text are recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the text may also be interpreted by Apple's on-device model. Images are never stored by the app, never uploaded and never sent to AudioLab or to any third party.

Discogs

To identify records and show market information, the app sends search requests to the Discogs API (api.discogs.com). These requests contain only the search terms derived from the scan or typed by you (for example a catalog number, label, artist or barcode) and the identifier of the release you open.

If you choose Sign in with Discogs, the login takes place on Discogs' own web page. The app never sees your Discogs password. Discogs returns an access token that is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and is used only to request Discogs suggested prices. You can sign out at any time in Settings, which deletes the token from the device.

Your use of Discogs is governed by Discogs' own terms and privacy policy, available on discogs.com. Wax Digger is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discogs.

Data stored on your device

  • Search history and watchlist (records you checked, the grade you chose and the asking price you entered), kept so you can review them offline.
  • App settings (currency, estimated shipping).
  • The Discogs access token, in the iOS Keychain, if you signed in.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to AudioLab. You can delete it at any time: Settings › Clear history, Settings › Sign out, or by deleting the app.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts on AudioLab servers (AudioLab has no servers for this app).
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • No collection of location, contacts, health or financial data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
